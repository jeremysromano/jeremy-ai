01 / See the work · PHH Mortgage

I go see the work before I design anything.

Before choosing a solution, we found where the work actually got stuck, by watching it happen.

Qualitative

Interviews

Loan officers, processors, underwriters and borrowers on where the work breaks down.

Quantitative

Time and motion

Followed live loans day by day, logging every touch, call and handoff.

Quantitative

Click-path analysis

Measured the clicks, screens and system hops each task required.

Both

System audit

Rated every application on usefulness, effectiveness, scalability and support.

We followed one live loan for 64 days and logged every touch

A Gemba walk: shadowing the loan team day by day, noting what helped, what hurt and why.

  • Expectations were not set upstream
  • Poor access to information created avoidable calls
  • Appraisal coordination became a handoff constraint
  • Simple changes required unnecessary effort

15 years of point solutions left us with a fragmented LOS

Data gaps, unclear status and manual workarounds turned the loan origination system itself into a constraint.

Conclusion: no single system fix would work. We redesigned around the tasks people actually do.

What's next · AI-first

Where I see the opportunity for lenders.

AI can take on the prep work so loan teams spend more time with borrowers. What I believe, what I've built, and where the opportunity is.

Belief 1

Start with the customer's goal. Remember it.

The goal and life event are the center, not the application. AI carries permission-based context across every interaction, product and life stage.

"Here's what we already know. Tell us what changed."

Belief 2

AI empowers the loan officer to present every viable option.

The 30-year fixed is no longer the only answer: 11%+ of rate locks are now non-QM, up from 1.4% in 2020; DSCR is a third of non-QM; 20% of homeowners hold rates under 3%. AI reads intent, evaluates eligibility and explains the trade-offs.

The AI advises the loan officer. The loan officer advises the customer.

Belief 3

Meet the next generation where they already are.

Instagram, TikTok, social video and whatever comes next. Loan officers, agents and creators become educators. AI researches, scripts, produces and personalizes content at scale: an always-on sales engine that builds familiarity and trust before the first conversation.

Belief 4

Instant underwriting becomes table stakes.

What once took processors, pre-underwriters and underwriters checking the same information again and again should happen as documents arrive. AI extracts, validates, verifies and flags exceptions in real time. People stay in the loop for escalations, judgment and final decisions, not for checking the checker.

People do business with people they know, like and trust. Insight and education earn that trust. AI-first, not AI bolted on.

  1. Ingest

    New regulatory and investor guidance comes in.

  2. Compare

    The model compares it against existing requirements.

  3. A person reviews

    Differences are flagged for a human to check and approve.

  4. Generate

    Approved changes become requirements, user stories and test cases.
